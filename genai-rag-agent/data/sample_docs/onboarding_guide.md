# Onboarding Guide

Most teams finish the initial setup in about 30 minutes. Start by creating a workspace, then invite your teammates and connect your support email channel.

## Importing contacts

You can import up to 50,000 contacts per CSV file. The file needs an email column; name, company and phone columns are optional. Rows with duplicate emails are merged automatically.

## Roles

Every member has one of four roles: Owner, Admin, Agent or Viewer. Owners manage billing and security settings, Admins manage users and workflows, Agents handle conversations, and Viewers have read-only access to reports.

## Training

We host live onboarding webinars every Tuesday at 10:00 UTC. Recordings are posted in the Help Center within one day. In your first week, we suggest connecting one channel, creating three saved replies and building one automation.
