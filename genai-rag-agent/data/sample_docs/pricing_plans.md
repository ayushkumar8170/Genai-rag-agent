# Pricing and Plans

NimbusDesk has three plans. Starter costs $15 per seat per month. Business costs $29 per seat per month and adds single sign-on, advanced reporting and a higher API limit. Enterprise has custom pricing and requires a minimum of 100 seats.

## Discounts and trials

Paying annually gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is required to start the trial.

## Add-ons

Extra storage costs $5 per 100 GB per month. The Premium support add-on costs $200 per month and includes a one-hour response time for urgent tickets and a named support engineer.
