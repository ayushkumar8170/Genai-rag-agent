# Refund Policy

NimbusDesk offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all new annual subscriptions. Customers who cancel within 30 days of the first payment receive a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Monthly subscriptions are not refundable after the billing date, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Exceptions

Add-on purchases such as extra storage packs or premium support hours are non-refundable once activated. Accounts suspended for violating the acceptable use policy are not eligible for a refund.

## How to request a refund

Open Billing, then Invoices, choose the invoice, and select Request refund. For disputes, email billing@nimbusdesk.example and include your invoice number. Our billing lead Anita Rao (anita.rao@nimbusdesk.example, +1 415 555 0132) reviews escalations within two business days.
