# Security and Data Privacy

Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Our SOC 2 Type II report is available to customers under NDA.

## Data residency and retention

Customers in the European Union can choose to have their data stored in our Frankfurt region. After an account is deleted, customer data is retained for 30 days to allow recovery and is then permanently purged. Audit logs are retained for 12 months.

## Access and identity

Single sign-on is available on the Business and Enterprise plans through SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Workspace owners can enforce two-factor authentication for every member.

## Use of customer content

NimbusDesk does not use customer content to train machine learning models. Sensitive fields such as phone numbers and email addresses can be masked with the field-level masking setting, which hides values from agents without the right permission.
