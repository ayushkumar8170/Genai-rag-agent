# Incident Response

We classify incidents by severity. A SEV1 is a full outage or data-loss risk and gets a response within 15 minutes, around the clock. A SEV2 is a major feature that is degraded and gets a response within 1 hour. A SEV3 is a minor issue and is handled by the next business day.

## Communication

During a SEV1 the status page is updated every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved. Affected customers are also notified by email. The support lead owns customer communication throughout the incident.

## After the incident

A written post-incident review is published within 5 business days for every SEV1 and SEV2. It describes the timeline, the root cause, and the actions we will take to prevent a repeat.
