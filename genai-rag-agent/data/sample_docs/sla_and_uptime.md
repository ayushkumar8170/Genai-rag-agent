# Service Level Agreement and Uptime

NimbusDesk guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for the Business and Enterprise plans and 99.5% for the Starter plan. Uptime is measured across the web application and the public API.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9% but stays at or above 99.0%, affected customers receive a credit of 10% of that month's subscription fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to the next invoice. Credits are not paid out as cash.

## Scheduled maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Maintenance inside this window does not count against uptime. We announce any maintenance outside this window at least 72 hours in advance on the status page.
